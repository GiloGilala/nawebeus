## 1. Executive Summary

This Product Roadmap defines the strategic direction and planned feature delivery for the Nawebeus platform from MVP launch (Q2 2026) through Year 3 (2028). The roadmap is organized into phases, each with clear objectives, key features, success metrics, and dependencies.

**Roadmap Philosophy:**
- **Customer-driven:** Features prioritized based on customer feedback and market demand
- **Value-focused:** Ship features that deliver measurable customer value
- **Iterative:** Continuous improvement based on usage data and feedback
- **Realistic:** Achievable with current team size and resources
- **Flexible:** Adaptable to market changes and new opportunities

**Key Milestones:**
- **Q2 2026:** MVP Launch (10 core modules)
- **Q3 2026:** Mobile App (iOS + Android)
- **Q4 2026:** Advanced AI/ML Features
- **Q1 2027:** Public API & Integrations
- **Q2 2027:** Enterprise Features (SSO, Advanced Workflows)
- **Q3 2027:** Additional Social Platforms (LinkedIn, TikTok, Pinterest)
- **Q4 2027:** Internationalization (Spanish, French, German)
- **Q1 2028:** White-Labeling & Agency Resale
- **Q2 2028:** Advanced Features (Influencer Management, Marketplace)

## 15. Feature Prioritization Framework

### 15.1 Prioritization Criteria

Features are evaluated and prioritized using the **RICE Framework**:

| Criterion | Description | Weight |
|-----------|-------------|--------|
| **Reach** | How many users will this impact? | 25% |
| **Impact** | How much will it impact those users? (0.25, 0.5, 1, 2, 3) | 30% |
| **Confidence** | How confident are we in our estimates? (0–100%) | 20% |
| **Effort** | How much effort is required? (person-months) | 25% |

**RICE Score = (Reach × Impact × Confidence) / Effort**

### 15.2 Additional Considerations

| Factor | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Strategic alignment** | Does it align with vision and strategy? |
| **Customer demand** | How many customers have requested this? |
| **Competitive parity** | Do competitors have this? |
| **Technical feasibility** | Can we build it with current tech? |
| **Revenue impact** | Will it increase revenue or retention? |
| **Risk** | What's the risk of building or not building? |

### 15.3 Prioritization Process

1. **Gather requests** from customers, sales, support, product team
2. **Score each request** using RICE framework
3. **Review with stakeholders** (product, engineering, design, sales)
4. **Make trade-off decisions** based on capacity and strategy
5. **Commit to roadmap** for next 2 quarters
6. **Review and adjust** quarterly

## 17. Risks & Mitigation

### 17.1 Roadmap Risks

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|------------|--------|------------|
| **Delayed delivery** | Medium | High | Buffer time in estimates; phased delivery; MVP mindset |
| **Scope creep** | High | Medium | Strict change control; quarterly reviews; clear acceptance criteria |
| **Market shifts** | Medium | High | Quarterly market reviews; flexible roadmap; customer feedback |
| **Technical debt** | Medium | Medium | Dedicated refactoring sprints; code quality standards |
| **Resource constraints** | Medium | High | Flexible team allocation; outsourcing for non-core; hiring plan |
| **Competitive pressure** | High | Medium | Differentiation; speed; customer lock-in |
| **Customer feedback changes priorities** | High | Medium | Regular customer interviews; quarterly roadmap review |
| **Platform TOS changes** | High | High | Diversification; abstraction layer; legal monitoring |

### 17.2 Roadmap Review Process

- **Monthly:** Tactical review (sprint-level progress)
- **Quarterly:** Strategic review (phase-level progress, market changes)
- **Annually:** Long-term vision review (3-year strategy)

**Review Questions:**
- Are we on track to meet phase objectives?
- Have market conditions changed?
- Have customer priorities shifted?
- Are resources adequate?
- Should we reprioritize upcoming work?
