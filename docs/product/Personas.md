## 1. Overview

This document defines the user personas for **Nawebeus** — a unified social media and PR management platform built for the African market. Personas represent the distinct user types who interact with the platform across enterprise brands, mid-market companies, and PR agencies.

These personas are living documents that guide product decisions, feature prioritization, UX design, engineering trade-offs, and marketing messaging. They are grounded in research, validated through user interviews, and updated as the product evolves.

Each persona includes:

- **Demographics:** Age, role, location, organization size
- **Goals:** What they are trying to achieve
- **Pain Points:** What frustrates or blocks them today
- **Behaviors:** How they work, which tools they use
- **Needs from Nawebeus:** What the platform must deliver for them
- **Success Metrics:** How they personally measure success
- **Financial Context:** Budget ownership, willingness to pay, cost sensitivity
- **Journey Map:** Their end-to-end experience with Nawebeus
