## 1. Executive Summary

Welcome to Nawebeus! This User Guide will help you get the most out of the platform, whether you're a marketing director looking for unified analytics, a social media manager running campaigns, or a community manager responding to messages. This guide covers all five core modules and provides step-by-step instructions for common tasks.

**What is Nawebeus?**

Nawebeus is a unified social media management and intelligence platform that combines five powerful capabilities in one platform:
- **Grow** — Run viral giveaway campaigns
- **Listen** — Monitor brand mentions and sentiment
- **Monitor** — Track media coverage and PR impact
- **Engage** — Manage all conversations from one inbox
- **Analyze** — Get actionable insights and reports

**Who is this guide for?**
- Marketing Directors (Maya persona)
- Social Media Managers (Sam persona)
- Agency Account Directors (Alex persona)
- PR Managers (Priya persona)

## 2. Getting Started

### 2.1 Creating Your Account

**Step 1: Sign Up**
1. Go to [nawebeus.com](https://nawebeus.com)
2. Click **"Start Free Trial"** or **"Sign Up"**
3. Enter your details:
   - Full name
   - Email address
   - Password (minimum 8 characters, with uppercase, lowercase, number, and special character)
   - Organization name
4. Accept the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy
5. Click **"Create Account"**

**Step 2: Verify Your Email**
1. Check your email inbox for a verification email from Nawebeus
2. Click the verification link in the email
3. You'll be automatically logged in and redirected to the onboarding wizard

**Step 3: Complete Onboarding**
The onboarding wizard will guide you through:
1. **Organization Setup** — Confirm your organization name, upload logo, set timezone
2. **Connect Your First Social Account** — Choose from YouTube, X, Instagram, Facebook, or Reddit
3. **Choose Your Focus** — Select what you want to do first (create a giveaway, set up monitoring, or explore dashboard)

**💡 Tip:** You can skip the wizard and explore on your own. You can always return to onboarding via the "Continue Setup" banner in your dashboard.

### 2.2 Navigating the Platform

**Main Navigation (Top Bar):**
- **Logo** — Click to return to dashboard
- **Dashboard** — Overview of all metrics
- **Grow** — Giveaway campaigns
- **Listen** — Social listening and mentions
- **Monitor** — Media monitoring
- **Engage** — Unified inbox
- **Analyze** — Analytics and reports
- **Notifications** — Alerts and updates
- **User Menu** — Settings, profile, help

**Sidebar (Within Modules):**
Each module has its own sidebar with module-specific sections.

**Mobile Navigation:**
On mobile devices, the navigation collapses into a hamburger menu (☰).

### 2.3 Connecting Social Media Accounts

**Step 1: Go to Settings**
1. Click your **user menu** (top right)
2. Select **"Settings"**
3. Click **"Social Accounts"** in the left sidebar

**Step 2: Connect an Account**
1. Click **"Connect Account"**
2. Select the platform (YouTube, X, Instagram, Facebook, or Reddit)
3. You'll be redirected to the platform's authorization page
4. Review the permissions Nawebeus is requesting
5. Click **"Authorize"** or **"Allow"**
6. You'll be redirected back to Nawebeus
7. The account will appear in your connected accounts list

**Permissions Explained:**
- **YouTube:** Read channel data, access comments, read analytics
- **X (Twitter):** Read tweets/mentions/DMs, post tweets, send DMs
- **Instagram:** Read profile, manage comments, manage DMs
- **Facebook:** List pages, read engagement, publish posts, manage messages
- **Reddit:** Read posts, submit posts, private messages

**Account Status:**
- 🟢 **Active** — Connected and working
- 🟡 **Needs Re-authentication** — Token expired; click to reconnect
- 🟠 **Rate Limited** — Temporarily throttled by platform
- 🔴 **Error** — Persistent issue; contact support
- ⚪ **Paused** — Manually paused

**💡 Tip:** Connect at least one social account to start seeing data in your dashboard.

## 4. Grow Module — Giveaway Campaigns

### 4.1 Creating Your First Giveaway

**Step 1: Start a New Campaign**
1. Click **"Grow"** in the top navigation
2. Click **"New Campaign"** or **"Create Campaign"**
3. Choose a starting point:
   - **Use a Template** — Pre-configured campaigns (recommended for beginners)
   - **Start from Scratch** — Full control over all settings

**Step 2: Basic Information**
1. **Campaign Name** (internal only) — e.g., "Black Friday Giveaway 2026"
2. **Campaign Title** (public-facing) — e.g., "Win an iPhone 15 Pro!"
3. **Description** — Explain the campaign, prize, how to enter
4. **Cover Image** — Upload an attractive image (JPG/PNG, max 5MB)
5. Click **"Next"**

**Step 3: Prize Details**
1. **Prize Name** — e.g., "iPhone 15 Pro Max"
2. **Prize Description** — Detailed description
3. **Prize Value** — Actual retail value (for tax/legal purposes)
4. **Prize Quantity** — How many winners
5. **Prize Images** — Upload product photos
6. **Shipping Required** — Yes/No
7. Click **"Next"**

**Step 4: Campaign Duration**
1. **Start Date/Time** — When campaign goes live
2. **End Date/Time** — When campaign ends (max 90 days)
3. **Timezone** — Select your timezone
4. Click **"Next"**

**Step 5: Entry Methods**
Choose how people can enter. Each method has a point value:

| Entry Method | Points | Description |
|--------------|--------|-------------|
| Follow Social Account | 1-10 | Follow your brand on a platform |
| Like Post | 2-5 | Like a specific post |
| Share Post | 5-15 | Share a post with their network |
| Comment on Post | 3-10 | Comment on a specific post |
| Visit Website | 5 | Visit your website (tracked via pixel) |
| Email Subscription | 10 | Subscribe to your email list |
| Refer Friends | 15 per referral | Invite friends who also enter |

**To add an entry method:**
1. Click **"Add Entry Method"**
2. Select the method type
3. Configure details (e.g., which post to like, which account to follow)
4. Set point value
5. Click **"Add"**
6. Repeat for additional methods
7. Click **"Next"**

**Step 6: Legal & Compliance**
1. **Terms & Conditions** — Use our template or write your own
2. **Minimum Age** — 13+, 16+, 18+, 21+, or custom
3. **Geographic Restrictions** — Select allowed countries (or "Worldwide")
4. **Tax Disclosures** — For prizes over ₦1.2M ($600), you may need to collect tax forms
5. **Privacy Policy** — Link to your privacy policy
6. Review the **Official Rules** (auto-generated)
7. Click **"Next"**

**Step 7: Preview**
1. Review your campaign in **Desktop** and **Mobile** preview
2. Test the entry flow
3. Make any final adjustments
4. Click **"Publish"** or **"Save as Draft"**

**Step 8: Publish**
1. Choose **"Publish Now"** or **"Schedule"**
2. If scheduling, select date and time
3. Click **"Confirm"**
4. Your campaign is live! 🎉

**You'll get:**
- A unique campaign URL: `nawebeus.com/g/your-org/campaign-slug`
- Embeddable widget code
- QR code for print materials
- Auto-posting to connected social accounts (if enabled)

### 4.2 Managing Campaigns

**Viewing Campaign Performance:**
1. Go to **Grow > Campaigns**
2. Click on a campaign to view details
3. See real-time metrics:
   - Total entries
   - Verified entries
   - Conversion rate
   - Geographic distribution

**Managing Entries:**
1. Go to **Grow > Entries**
2. Filter by status (verified, pending, disqualified)
3. View participant details
4. Approve or disqualify entries

**Selecting Winners:**
1. Go to your campaign

   - **Weighted Random** — Higher points = better chance

6. Click **"Select Winners"**
7. System uses cryptographically secure random selection
8. Winners are automatically notified via email

**Prize Fulfillment:**
1. Track winner responses in the campaign dashboard
2. For unclaimed prizes, system automatically promotes runner-ups
3. Collect shipping addresses from winners
4. Update prize status (shipped, delivered)
5. Add tracking numbers
6. Winners confirm receipt

## 5. Listen Module — Social Listening

### 5.1 Creating Your First Listening Query

**Step 1: Go to Listen Module**
1. Click **"Listen"** in the top navigation
2. Click **"New Query"** or **"Create Query"**

**Step 2: Define Keywords**
Use the visual query builder to create your search:

**Basic Keywords:**
- Type your brand name (e.g., "Acme Corp")
- Add variations: "Acme Corporation", "@acmecorp", "#acme"
- Use boolean operators: AND, OR, NOT

**Advanced Search:**
- **Exact Match:** Use quotes — `"customer service"`
- **OR:** `brand OR product` (either term)
- **AND:** `brand AND launch` (both required)
- **NOT:** `brand NOT competitor` (exclude term)
- **NEAR:** `CEO NEAR/3 resignation` (within 3 words)
- **Parentheses:** `(brand OR product) AND (launch OR release)`

**Example Queries:**
- Brand Monitoring: `(Acme Corp OR "Acme Corporation" OR @acmecorp) AND (review OR feedback) NOT competitor`
- Crisis Detection: `Acme AND (complaint OR problem OR terrible) NEAR/3 service`
- Competitor Analysis: `(CompetitorA OR CompetitorB) AND (vs OR compared OR alternative)`

**Step 3: Select Platforms**
Choose which platforms to monitor:
- ☑️ YouTube
- ☑️ X (Twitter)
- ☑️ Instagram
- ☑️ Facebook
- ☑️ Reddit
- ☑️ News/Blogs

**Step 4: Advanced Filters**
- **Languages:** Select languages (default: English)
- **Countries:** Filter by geographic location
- **Minimum Engagement:** Set threshold (e.g., 10+ likes)
- **Minimum Influence Score:** Filter by author influence (0-100)
- **Author Filters:** Verified accounts only, influencers only

**Step 5: Save Query**
1. Name your query (e.g., "Brand Mentions - Acme Corp")
2. Add description
3. Click **"Save"**
4. Your query starts collecting mentions immediately!

**Estimated Volume:**
Before saving, you'll see an estimated number of mentions per month based on your keywords and filters.

### 5.2 Understanding Your Mention Feed

**Mention Feed Components:**

**Mention Card shows:**
- Author avatar and name
- Platform icon
- Full mention content
- Sentiment badge (🟢 Positive | ⚪ Neutral | 🔴 Negative)
- Influence score (0-100)
- Engagement metrics (likes, shares, comments)
- Timestamp
- Actions (Reply, Tag, Hide, Share)

**Filtering Mentions:**
1. Click **"Filters"** (top right)
2. Filter by:
   - Platform
   - Sentiment
   - Date range
   - Influence score
   - Tags
   - Query
3. Click **"Apply"**

**Searching Mentions:**
- Use the search bar (top)
- Search by keyword, author, or content
- Supports advanced search operators

**Tagging Mentions:**
1. Click on a mention
2. Click **"Tags"**
3. Add tags (e.g., "complaint", "praise", "question")
4. Click **"Save"**

**Hiding Mentions:**
1. Click **"Hide"** on a mention
2. Mention is excluded from analytics and feed
3. View hidden mentions: **Filters > Show Hidden**

### 5.3 Setting Up Alerts

**Creating an Alert:**
1. Go to **Listen > Alerts**
2. Click **"New Alert"**
3. Choose alert type:
   - **Volume Spike** — Sudden increase in mentions (5x normal)
   - **Sentiment Crash** — Negative sentiment spike
   - **Influencer Mention** — High-influence author mentions you
   - **Crisis Keyword** — Specific crisis terms detected
   - **Competitive Mention** — Competitor mentioned
4. Configure conditions:
   - **Threshold:** How much change triggers alert
   - **Queries:** Which queries to monitor
5. Choose notification channels:
   - ☑️ Email
   - ☑️ In-app
   - ☐ Mobile push (future)
   - ☐ Slack/Teams (future)
6. Add recipients (email addresses)
7. Configure quiet hours (e.g., 10 PM - 8 AM)
8. Click **"Save"**

**Alert Notifications:**
When triggered, you'll receive:
- Email with mention details
- In-app notification badge
- Link to view all related mentions

### 5.4 Analytics & Reports

**Viewing Sentiment Trends:**
1. Go to **Listen > Analytics**
2. Select time range
3. View charts:
   - Sentiment over time (line chart)
   - Sentiment distribution (pie chart)
   - Volume trends
   - Top authors by reach
   - Geographic distribution
   - Platform performance

**Share of Voice (SOV) Report:**
1. Go to **Listen > SOV**
2. Select time range
3. Add competitors (up to 50 on Enterprise)
4. View comparison:
   - Your SOV vs. competitors
   - Sentiment comparison
   - Reach comparison
   - Platform-by-platform breakdown

**Exporting Data:**
1. Go to **Listen > Export**
2. Select data (mentions, analytics)
3. Choose format: CSV, Excel, PDF
4. Select date range and filters
5. Click **"Export"**
6. Download link sent to your email

### 6.5 Crisis Management

**Detecting a Crisis:**
The system automatically detects crises based on:
- Sudden spike in negative coverage (5x+ normal)
- Tier-1 outlet negative coverage
- Influencer amplification
- Crisis keyword detection
- Cross-platform spread

**Crisis Severity Levels:**
- **Level 1 (Minor):** Isolated negative mention — Response <4 hours
- **Level 2 (Moderate):** Multiple negative articles — Response <2 hours
- **Level 3 (Significant):** Tier-1 coverage — Response <1 hour
- **Level 4 (Severe):** Multiple tier-1 outlets — Response <30 minutes
- **Level 5 (Critical):** Mainstream coverage — Immediate response

**Crisis Dashboard:**
When a crisis is detected, you get:
- Real-time coverage timeline
- Sentiment trend chart
- Top mentions
- Affected platforms
- Stakeholder notification panel

**Responding to a Crisis:**
1. Review the crisis dashboard
2. Assess severity and impact
3. Notify stakeholders per your crisis playbook
4. Use response templates (holding statement, denial, apology)
5. Distribute response to media
6. Monitor response impact in real-time
7. Document lessons learned post-crisis

| Priority | First Response | Resolution |
|----------|----------------|------------|
| Urgent | 15 minutes | 4 hours |

## 12. Troubleshooting

### 12.1 Common Issues

**Issue: I can't log in**
- ✅ Check that you're using the correct email and password
- ✅ Reset your password if needed
- ✅ Check if Caps Lock is on
- ✅ Clear browser cookies and try again
- ✅ Contact your Admin if you're locked out

**Issue: Social account shows "Needs Re-authentication"**
- ✅ Click the account and select **"Re-authenticate"**
- ✅ Log in to the platform and grant permissions again
- ✅ If the issue persists, disconnect and reconnect the account

**Issue: Not seeing data in dashboard**
- ✅ Make sure you've connected at least one social account
- ✅ Check that the account is **Active** (green status)
- ✅ Wait 15-30 minutes for initial data sync
- ✅ Verify the date range includes recent data

**Issue: Can't create a campaign**
- ✅ Check that you're on the Pro or Enterprise plan
- ✅ Verify you have Admin or Manager role
- ✅ Ensure all required fields are filled
- ✅ Check that prize value and quantity are valid
- ✅ Review legal terms and compliance

**Issue: Export failed**
- ✅ Check that the date range is valid
- ✅ Verify you have permission to export data
- ✅ Try a smaller date range or fewer records
- ✅ Contact support if the issue persists

**Issue: Notifications not working**
- ✅ Check notification settings (Settings > Notifications)
- ✅ Verify email address is correct
- ✅ Check spam/junk folder
- ✅ Disable quiet hours if needed
- ✅ Whitelist `noreply@nawebeus.com`

**Issue: Slow performance**
- ✅ Clear browser cache and cookies
- ✅ Try a different browser
- ✅ Check your internet connection
- ✅ Reduce the number of open browser tabs
- ✅ Try during off-peak hours

## 13. Glossary

**API (Application Programming Interface):** A set of rules and protocols for building and interacting with software applications.

**AVE (Advertising Value Equivalence):** A metric that estimates the cost of equivalent paid advertising for media coverage.

**CSAT (Customer Satisfaction Score):** A measure of customer satisfaction, typically on a 1-5 scale.

**CTA (Call to Action):** A prompt for the user to take a specific action (e.g., "Sign up now").

**Engagement Rate:** The percentage of your audience that interacts with your content (likes, comments, shares, clicks).

**Hashtag:** A keyword or phrase preceded by "#" used to categorize content on social media.

**KPI (Key Performance Indicator):** A measurable value that demonstrates how effectively a company is achieving key business objectives.

**MFA (Multi-Factor Authentication):** A security method that requires two or more verification factors to access an account.

**Mention:** A social media post that references your brand, product, or keyword.

**Multi-Tenant:** A software architecture where a single instance serves multiple customers (tenants).

**Reach:** The total number of unique users who saw your content.

**ROI (Return on Investment):** A performance measure used to evaluate the efficiency of an investment.

**Sentiment Analysis:** The process of determining the emotional tone (positive, neutral, negative) of a piece of text.

**Share of Voice (SOV):** A metric that measures your brand's share of the total conversation in your industry.

**SLA (Service Level Agreement):** A commitment between a service provider and a client about performance standards.

**UGC (User-Generated Content):** Content created by users (customers, fans) about your brand.

**Webhook:** A way for an application to send automated messages or data to another application in real-time.
